The fifth contest was a Full Metal Mayhem match for a future match on Impact ! for the NWA World Heavyweight Championship . Early in the match , Abyss placed two tables side @-@ by @-@ side and then stacked two more on top of those at ringside . Mid @-@ way through the match , Hardy grabbed Abyss and placed him on a table at the top of the entrance ramp , he then ascended to the roof of the entrance ramp and performed a front @-@ flip , landing on Abyss in a move Hardy calls the Swanton Bomb . In the final minutes of the match , Hardy climbed a ladder and retrieved one of the envelopes , however , it was empty which meant the last one held the future championship match . Hardy then tried to move the ladder and retrieve the second envelope . While he was climbing again , Abyss grabbed him and pulled him down , he then threw Hardy against the ropes . When Hardy connected with the ropes , he flipped over them and crashed through the four tables set up earlier in the match . Abyss followed by climbing the ladder and removing the envelope to win the match .
