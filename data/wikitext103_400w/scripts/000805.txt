Users have also reported numerous problems after receiving the update including the accessibility features and battery @-@ related issues . Many users predicted that the iPad 2 was going to be dropped from updating to iOS 8 along with the iPhone 4 , the same process that happened with the iPhone 3GS and the iPod Touch ( fourth generation ) and earlier models , considering that it has been supported for more than 3 years . It was confirmed on June 8 , 2015 , at Apple 's WWDC that the iPad 2 would support iOS 9 . This makes the iPad 2 the first iPad and only iOS device to support six major versions of iOS .
