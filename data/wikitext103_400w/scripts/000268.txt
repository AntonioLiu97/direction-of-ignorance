On this basis , the JTWC estimated that the storm attained peak winds of 75 km / h ( 45 mph ) . However , a nearby ship reported sustained winds of 85 km / h ( 50 mph ) , and the well @-@ defined structure on satellite imagery suggested winds as strong as 120 km / h ( 75 mph ) . Moving northwestward , the cyclone made landfall around 09 : 00 UTC on June 11 about 130 km ( 80 mi ) southwest of Masirah Island in southeastern Oman , at a location named Ras Madrakah . It quickly weakened over the desert terrain and dry air , and the circulation dissipated by June 12 over the central portion of the country . However , the remnants turned to the southwest , steered by a northerly flow . It entered the Rub ' al Khali , or Empty Quarter , of Saudi Arabia late on June 12 , and continued slowly westward . The storm 's interaction with the monsoon brought the intertropical convergence zone northward into Oman and Yemen , bringing unusually heavy rainfall until the system gradually wound down . The IMD – the official warning agency for the basin – did not track the cyclone . In general , tropical cyclone forecast models failed to predict that the storm would form . The precursor to the storm dropped heavy rainfall in Oman , reaching 29 mm ( 1 @.@ 1 in ) in Khaftawt on May 31 . The storm later produced intense precipitation across the coast and desert regions of the country . Masirah recorded 48 mm ( 1 @.@ 9 in ) of rainfall over 36 hours , compared to the average monthly average of 1 mm ( 0 @.@ 039 in ) , while Salalah reported 36 mm ( 1 @.@ 4 in ) , or 600 % of the average June rainfall .
