McMahon to show appreciation for Dudley 's contributions to the company . Dudley has been described as an " important cog " in the company , and McMahon once stated that " had there been no James Dudley , the WWF possibly wouldn 't exist as it does today " . Dudley continued to feel a sense of loyalty to the McMahons and their promotion . Dudley was inducted into the WWF Hall of Fame in 1994 by Vincent K. McMahon . Dudley 's final appearance with the company came during the January 4 , 2002 episode of SmackDown ! . According to the storyline , Stephanie McMahon was banned from the MCI Center . In an attempt to get past security , she pushed Dudley in a wheelchair but was still refused entrance . Following the scene , Dudley left the wheelchair , walked to his seat , and watched the show . Dudley continued to live in the District of Columbia after retiring from professional wrestling . He had 37 grandchildren , 34 great @-@ grandchildren , and 16 great @-@ great @-@ grandchildren . Dudley died of natural causes on June 1 , 2004 at the age of 94 .
