Established in 1769 by Eleazar Wheelock , Dartmouth is one of the nine colonial colleges founded before the American Revolution . Following a liberal arts curriculum , the university provides undergraduate instruction in 40 academic departments and interdisciplinary programs including 57 majors in the humanities , social sciences , natural sciences , and engineering , and enables students to design specialized majors and minors or engage in dual degree programs . Dartmouth comprises five constituent schools : the original undergraduate college , the Geisel School of Medicine , the Thayer School of Engineering , the Tuck School of Business , and the School of Graduate and Advanced Studies .
