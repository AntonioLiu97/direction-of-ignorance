The show centers on FBI special agents Fox Mulder ( David Duchovny ) and Dana Scully ( Gillian Anderson ) who work on cases linked to the paranormal , called X @-@ Files . Mulder is a believer in the paranormal , while the skeptical Scully has been assigned to debunk his work . In this episode , The Lone Gunmen run into the enigmatic Susanne Modeski . After deceiving Scully into joining them , the trio soon find out that Susanne 's fiancé is planning to use her new brainwashing drug for political assassinations . " Three of a Kind " functions as a sequel of sorts to the fifth season episode " Unusual Suspects " , concluding the story of The Lone Gunmen and Susanne Modeski , the woman who led to the creation of the trio .
