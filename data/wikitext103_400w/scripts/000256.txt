Hurricane Erin was the longest @-@ lived hurricane in the 2001 Atlantic hurricane season . The sixth tropical depression , fifth tropical storm , and first hurricane , Erin developed from a tropical wave on September 1 . After strengthening to a 60 mph ( 95 km / h ) tropical storm as it moved to the west @-@ northwest , wind shear weakened Erin and caused it to dissipate on September 5 . The remnants re @-@ organized into a tropical depression the next day , and Erin strengthened into a hurricane on September 9 while moving northwestward .
