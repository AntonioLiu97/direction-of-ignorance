A robber bursts into the store and shoots at Woods , but Apu saves him by jumping in front of the bullet . At the hospital , Dr. Hibbert says that Apu survived because the bullet ricocheted off another bullet that was lodged in his chest from a previous robbery . Eternally grateful , Woods gives Apu his job back , and goes away to fight against extraterrestrial beings in his next ' movie ' . The episode was written by Greg Daniels , and directed by Mark Kirkland . It was the first full episode of the show that Daniels wrote . The Simpsons writers Al Jean and Mike Reiss , who were show runners during the previous two seasons , came up with the idea for the episode . They left the idea with David Mirkin , who took over the job as show runner during this season . Mirkin said he was " very excited and intrigued " with the idea of the episode .
