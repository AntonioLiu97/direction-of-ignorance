Subtraction of two vectors and division by a ( non @-@ zero ) scalar can be defined as v − w = v + ( − w ) , v / a = ( 1 / a ) v. When the scalar field F is the real numbers R , the vector space is called a real vector space . When the scalar field is the complex numbers , it is called a complex vector space . These two cases are the ones used most often in engineering . The general definition of a vector space allows scalars to be elements of any fixed field F. The notion is then known as an F @-@ vector spaces or a vector space over F. A field is , essentially , a set of numbers possessing addition , subtraction , multiplication and division operations .
