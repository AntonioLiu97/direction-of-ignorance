William Holden as Ransome – A CIA agent who accompanies M to persuade Bond out of retirement , then reappears in the final climactic fight scene . Charles Boyer as LeGrand – A Deuxième Bureau agent who accompanies M and Ransom to see Bond . Geoffrey Bayldon as Q.
