Newark is the home of the multiple institutions of higher education , including : a Berkeley College campus , the main campus of Essex County College , New Jersey Institute of Technology ( NJIT ) , the Newark Campus of Rutgers Biomedical and Health Sciences ( formerly University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey ) , Rutgers University – Newark and Seton Hall University School of Law . Most of Newark 's academic institutions are located in the city 's University Heights district . The colleges and universities have worked together to help revitalize the area , which serves more than 50 @,@ 000 students and faculty .
